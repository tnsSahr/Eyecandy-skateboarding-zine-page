Street

1990s Heritage

With a new decade, skateboarding changed yet again. Street skating became the dominant art form: the urban environment flooded with rule-breaking skaters embracing all handrails, stairs, and marble curbs.

CORE I: INTERACTION · FALL 2026 TOMPKINS / BROOKLYN BANKS

Culture

The updated skating style and 'street culture' created a mix that influenced the next generation of skaters and graphic designers.

The rise in learning technical curb tricks brought the need for more durable and comfortable clothes: thick cotton fleeces, flannel jackets, big denim, and heavyweight 7-ply Canadian hard rock maple decks.

EyeCandy translates this seasonal street ethos into hardware and print objects designed to withstand asphalt and cold rain.
